# Tailored Moto — Brand Guide

## Positioning

**White-glove handlebar fitting for Harley owners who value their time.**

Tailored Moto is not a repair shop. It is a fitting service — like a tailor for your bike.
The customer is an affluent Harley owner who could wrench it themselves or wait two weeks
at the dealership, but would rather have it done perfectly, conveniently, and without
touching a tool. Price signals quality; the experience signals ease.

What we sell: *the result and the convenience*, never the labor hour.

## Voice

- **Confident, calm, unhurried.** Short sentences. No exclamation points, no hype words
  ("amazing", "best", "cheap", "deal").
- Speak to the rider's taste and time, not to specs. Torque values build trust in the
  details; they never lead.
- "Fitting", "fitment", "dialed in", "turnkey" — yes. "Install job", "wrenching",
  "hog", "sled" — no.
- Always "your Harley" / "your bike", never "the vehicle".

## Taglines (options)

1. **Bars, fitted. Nothing else touched.** (precision + trust)
2. **The ride you pictured. Without the weekend in the garage.** (convenience)
3. **Handlebars, tailored.** (short, on-name)

## Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Ink (backgrounds, text) | Near-black | `#141210` |
| Ivory (light bg, text on dark) | Warm ivory | `#F7F3EC` |
| Brass (accents, CTAs, rules) | Brushed brass | `#B08D57` |
| Brass bright (hover) | Polished brass | `#C9A468` |
| Chrome (secondary text) | Muted gray | `#8D8A84` |

Deliberately **no Harley orange** — orange says dealership and merchandise;
black/ivory/brass says private atelier.

## Typography

- **Display / headings:** Playfair Display (serif, high contrast) — 500 & 600 weights.
- **Body / UI:** Inter — 400 & 600 weights.
- Headlines in sentence case or small-caps with wide letter-spacing. Never bold-shout.

## Logo

- **Wordmark:** "TAILORED MOTO" in Playfair Display, letterspaced small caps, with a
  thin brass rule and the word "MOTO" in brass. Used in the site header (HTML/CSS) and
  `logo.svg`.
- **Monogram / favicon:** geometric handlebar glyph (a drag-bar curve with risers) in
  brass on near-black rounded square — reads at 16 px where letters would not.
  See `favicon.svg`.

## Imagery direction (for later photos)

Dark, low-key garage lighting; shallow depth of field; chrome and leather close-ups;
hands with clean gloves. Never fluorescent shop lighting, never cluttered benches.
