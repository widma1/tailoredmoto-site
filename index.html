Your fitter

Handlebars look simple. Fitment isn't.

Riser torque, cable reach, internal wiring, brake line routing, bar angle to your shoulders — small decisions that separate a clean fit from a constant annoyance. Tailored Moto exists to make those decisions correctly, once, so you never think about them again.

Founded by Vidmantas Steponavicius, a mechanic who has spent years making machines feel right — now focused on one thing: Harleys, fitted to their riders.

The practical stuff

How long will you have the bike?

Days, not weeks. You'll get a firm timeline with your quote, before you commit to anything.

Where does the work happen?

Usually in Stow — you'll get the address and a drop-off time when you book. Too far? Ask about pickup and delivery, or we can come do the work at your place.

Is the work guaranteed?

If anything we touched isn't right, bring it back and we'll make it right. No fine print.

Do you only work on Harleys?

Harleys are the specialty. Riding something else? Ask — if it's not work we can do to our standard, we'll tell you straight.

Do I need to know which fitting I need?

No. Text a photo of your bike and the bars you're picturing — we'll tell you which fit it is and quote it.
